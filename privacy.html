Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. This page explains, plainly, what Connectus Flashcards collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and a display name if you set one.
  • Your study progress: which cards you rated, the rating you gave, the review interval you chose, each card's next-due date, any edits or personal notes you write, and any cards you delete.
  • Images you attach: any picture you add to a card is stored with that card so it shows on your devices.
  • Your last-opened deck and cycle count, so the home screen can show what you were practising.
  • Payment reference: when you buy a pass, we store the order reference, the pass and the amount — never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Anonymous usage counters: simple counts such as a visit, a sign-up, a payment, a deck open. These are numbers, not profiles.

Why we collect it

  • to run the app and show your decks and progress;
  • to sync your progress across the devices you sign in on;
  • to unlock the decks your pass covers;
  • to understand which parts of the app are used, so we can improve it.

Where it is stored

Your account and progress are stored in our database (Supabase) and are tied to your account. Your progress also syncs across every device you sign in on with the same email. On the device itself, the app keeps a copy in your browser's local storage so it works offline.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card / UPI / bank details go to Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us. We only receive a confirmation that a payment succeeded, with its reference.

Who we share with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers only to run the app: Supabase (database and sign-in), Razorpay (payments) and our web host. They process the data on our instructions.

Cookies and local storage

The app does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to store your progress on the device.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time, and clear the device copy from your browser settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it — email us and we will do it.
  • You can ask what we hold about you.

Security and retention

We keep your data while your account exists, and delete it when you ask us to close the account (except records we must keep, such as payment references for accounting). Access to the database is restricted; paid content is served only to entitled accounts.

Children

The app is meant for adult and professional study. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
