Refund & Cancellation

Effective 10 October 2026. This policy explains when a pass is refundable.

Digital product

A pass is a digital product that unlocks instantly, so it is consumed as soon as it is used. That is why the refund window below is short and tied to whether you have started using it.

Full refund within 24 hours (unused)

If you have not opened any deck after buying a pass, you can ask for a full refund within 24 hours of the payment. We will refund the full amount to your original payment method.

After that

Once 24 hours have passed, or once you have opened any deck with that pass, the pass is considered used and is not refundable.

Failed or duplicate payments

If a payment fails but money left your account, or you were charged twice for the same pass, we will refund it in full — just contact us with the payment reference.

Cancellation and renewal

Passes do not auto-renew. A pass simply expires at the end of its period, and there is nothing to cancel. You can keep using the free decks after it expires.

How to request a refund

  • Email life.rknain@gmail.com from the address on your account.
  • Include the payment reference / order id and the date of payment.
  • We aim to reply within 2 working days, and refunds reach the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.

Contact

Any question about a payment or refund: life.rknain@gmail.com.
